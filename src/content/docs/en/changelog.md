The small badge next to the logo shows which version of the site you are reading. This page
lists the changes, newest first.

## 2026-10-30: vinyl cutter guide pictures

- The vinyl cutter guide is illustrated: example photo and diagrams (rollers, blade, test cut, weeding).
- 3D printing guide: other slicers allowed, firmware to check regularly, safety glasses when removing supports.

## 2026-10-30: 3D printing guide reviewed

- The 3D printing guide is simplified, illustrated with pictures from the Prusa handbook, and links to the latest PrusaSlicer and firmware.
- The vinyl cutter guide links to the machine's presentation page.

## 2026-10-30: vinyl cutter guide reviewed

- The vinyl cutter guide is simplified and uses Graphtec Studio 2 (with its download link).

## 2026-10-30: vinyl cutter and 3D printing guides

- New full guide for the Graphtec CE6000-60 Plus cutting plotter and Graphtec Studio.
- New full guide for the Prusa MK4 3D printer and PrusaSlicer 2.9.6.
- The homepage presents these two machines like the others.

## 2026-10-30: new homepage

- The homepage now presents the FabLab machines, the safety rules to follow and the
  common outline of the guides (in French and English).

## 2026-10-29: new version of the site

- The site moves to the latest Astro and Starlight.
- Guide images open full screen with a click.
- Diagrams (Mermaid) are drawn when the site is built, with an "Enlarge" button to zoom them.
- The site version is shown next to the logo.
- The guides' content is unchanged.
