## Before using a machine

- Read the machine's guide in full before using it for the first time.
- Wear the protection listed in the guide: safety glasses, hearing protection, etc.
- Never leave a running machine unattended.
- Clean the machine and your workspace after each use.
- At all times, please check with the RoboTic workshop trainer before starting a machine.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<details>
<summary>Who can use the workshop machines?</summary>

Any use by a participant requires the prior agreement of the RoboTic workshop trainer. Always check with them before starting a machine, even if you have used it before.

</details>
<details>
<summary>Which machine should I start with?</summary>

3D printing, laser engraving and vinyl cutting are suited to the **Beginner** level; CNC milling is **Advanced** level. We therefore recommend starting with a beginner machine and carefully reading its whole guide before using it for the first time.

</details>
<details>
<summary>Can I work on my own projects?</summary>

**Yes, it is entirely possible and even encouraged!**

Please note, however, that customer orders take priority over personal or group projects. Remember to check your project's goals and feasibility with your trainer beforehand.

*Note: the materials used for your personal projects are at your own expense (they will be billed to you).*

</details>
<details>
<summary>Can I bring my own materials and equipment?</summary>

- **Materials:** yes, as long as they are compatible with the machines and approved by the person in charge of the workshop. If in doubt, always ask the RoboTic trainer to approve them.
- **Equipment:** you are welcome to bring your own work equipment if needed (laptop, tablet, etc.).

</details>
<details>
<summary>Can I leave a machine running on its own?</summary>

**No, never unattended.** You must stay close to the machine for as long as it is running. If a 3D print lasts longer than your planned time at the workshop, it must be authorised in advance by the person in charge.

</details>
<details>
<summary>What should I do if there is a problem or an emergency?</summary>

1. **Equipment incident:** stop the machine immediately (emergency stop button or power switch) and tell the RoboTic workshop trainer straight away.
2. **Emergency / uncontrollable fire:** evacuate the premises, set off the fire alarm and call emergency services (**911**).

*Reminder: the safety sheets are at the end of each technical guide.*

</details>
<details>
<summary>Can I ask an AI questions about a guide?</summary>

Yes. Just click the **“Copy page”** button (on the right of the guide's title) and paste the content into the AI tool of your choice.

</details>
<details>
<summary>How can I buy the items made at the workshop?</summary>

Go straight to our **<a href="https://online.forms.app/boutique-chouette-et-cie/formulaire-de-commerce?formsRef=qrcode" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Vert l’emploi</a>** online shop. As a workshop participant, you get a **25%** discount on the displayed price.

</details>
</Faq>
