## Before using a machine

- Read the machine's guide in full before using it for the first time.
- Wear the protection listed in the guide: safety glasses, hearing protection, etc.
- Never leave a running machine unattended.
- Clean the machine and your workspace after each use.
- At all times, please check with the RoboTic workshop trainer before starting a machine.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<details>
<summary>Who can use the machines in the workshop?</summary>

Every use needs the agreement of the RoboTic workshop trainer. Always check with them before starting a machine, even one you have used before.

</details>
<details>
<summary>Which machine should I start with?</summary>

3D printing, laser engraving and vinyl cutting are **Beginner** level; the CNC machine is **Advanced** level. Start with a Beginner machine and read its whole guide before using it for the first time.

</details>
<details>
<summary>Which machine fits my project?</summary>

- A plastic object: [3D Printing](/pae-docs/en/guides/impression-3d/).
- Engraving wood, cardboard, leather or fabric, or cutting 3 mm wood: [Laser Engraver](/pae-docs/en/guides/gravure-laser/).
- Stickers, lettering or heat-transfer vinyl: [Vinyl Cutter](/pae-docs/en/guides/decoupe-vinyle/).
- Milling or cutting wood, or making printed circuit boards: [CNC Machine](/pae-docs/en/guides/cnc-machine/).

</details>
<details>
<summary>Can I bring my own materials?</summary>

Only compatible materials approved by the person in charge of the room. If in doubt, ask before you start. Never engrave PVC or vinyl with the laser: their fumes are toxic.

</details>
<details>
<summary>Can I leave a machine running on its own?</summary>

No. Stay next to the machine for the whole job. A 3D print that lasts longer than your time in the room must be approved by the person in charge.

</details>
<details>
<summary>What should I do if something goes wrong?</summary>

Stop the machine (emergency stop button or switch off the power) and tell the person in charge. If a fire gets out of control: evacuate, set off the fire alarm and call emergency services (911). Each machine's instructions are in its **safety sheet**, at the end of its guide.

</details>
<details>
<summary>How do I print a guide or its safety sheet?</summary>

In a guide, open the menu on the right of the title and choose **Print page**: the guide prints without the menus. The safety sheet, at the end of the guide, has its own **Print** button: it fits on one A4 page, to post next to the machine.

</details>
<details>
<summary>Can I ask an AI questions about a guide?</summary>

Yes. The **Copy page** button, on the right of a guide's title, copies the guide so you can paste it into an AI tool. The menu next to it also offers **Open in Claude** and **Open in ChatGPT**.

</details>
<details>
<summary>How can I buy the items made in the workshop?</summary>

On our <a href="https://online.forms.app/boutique-chouette-et-cie/formulaire-de-commerce?formsRef=qrcode" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Vert l'emploi online shop</a>.

</details>
</Faq>
